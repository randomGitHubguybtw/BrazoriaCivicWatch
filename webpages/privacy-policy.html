Last Updated: August 14, 2026

Brazoria Civic Watch ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to providing transparent, non-partisan civic engagement resources, public meeting coverage, and community polling for the residents of Brazoria County, Texas. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, as well as publicly available information necessary to conduct community research:

Contact Information: Email addresses, phone numbers, and names submitted when signing up for our mailing list, contacting us, volunteering, or donating.

Polling & Survey Data: Candidate preferences, policy opinions, and optional self-reported demographic information (such as age range, general precinct/city of residence, and education level).

Public Voter Records: Publicly available voter registration data (such as voting district/precinct) sourced directly from official government election records in compliance with Texas Election Code § 18.066.

Technical & Log Data: Standard, non-identifying technical data including IP addresses, browser types, referring pages, and device information automatically recorded when visiting our website.

We use collected data strictly for non-partisan civic education and research purposes:

• To deliver newsletters, meeting summaries, and candidate coverage you requested via email.

• To administer independent community surveys and weight statistical polling samples accurately across Brazoria County demographics.

• To publish aggregated, anonymized polling trends and civic statistics on our website and in press releases.

• To process non-profit donations and manage volunteer initiatives.

Data Privacy, Anonymity, and Third Parties

Never Sold or Rented: We do not sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal contact information or survey responses to third parties, commercial advertisers, or partisan campaigns.

Strict Polling Anonymity: When you participate in a poll or survey, your specific individual vote is decoupled from your personal contact details. Only aggregated, de-identified data (e.g., overall demographic percentages) is published or shared.

Service Providers: We may share necessary contact information with trusted technical service providers (such as email delivery platforms, SMS APIs, and hosting providers) solely to perform services on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.

SMS & Mobile Data Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with or sold to any third parties.

Message frequency varies. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for assistance.

You have complete control over how and when you hear from us:

Email Communications: Every automated email sent through our newsletter engine contains an explicit "Unsubscribe" link in the footer. You may opt out of future emails at any time.

SMS & Phone Outreach: If you receive a text message or survey call from us, you may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message or indicating your preference to our volunteer/automated system. Upon receiving an opt-out request, your number will be permanently added to our internal suppression list.

Our platform and surveys are intended for eligible voters and general community audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational procedures or applicable laws. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
